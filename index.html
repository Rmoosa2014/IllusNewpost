02 — First Post: Two Directions

Introducing Ai Influencer Post, or without Influencer.

We present two distinct approaches for the launch post:

Option A: With an AI Influencer Character
Integrates a bespoke AI actor into the visual design. The character portrays relatable human emotions, commands viewer attention, boosts engagement, and forms a direct real-life connection paired with a striking, hook-driven caption.

Option B: Without a Character (Product-Led)
A clean, minimalist approach focusing purely on the product imagery and a powerful hook caption to drive the message home directly.

The AI Influencer Advantage

For advanced brand content, we can create AI influencers—whether it's a woman, a man, or an entire family—and use them across all posts with suitable characters tailored to the base of the product behavior. This allows us to produce dynamic story videos and authentic UGC videos effortlessly.

This unlocks a highly scalable workflow for authentic, UGC-style marketing at a fraction of traditional photoshoot costs, ensuring perfect brand consistency across all future campaigns.

06 — The Signature Pattern

Introducing the new pattern.

The pattern is simple and directly related to ILLUS product lights, LED tracks, and electrical connections. It also serves as a subtle watermark, with the brand name cleverly blended and embedded directly within the pattern shapes.

We can use the pattern and logo together—as showcased in the footer design—across all four posts to maintain a strong, unified visual consistency and distinct brand identity.

The Hidden ILLUS text

When activated with the brand's signature red accent nodes, a dynamic chase sequence runs through the pattern, illuminating the hidden "ILLUS" wordmark within the node connections.

07 — Layout & Composition

Introducing margin on the post.

Introducing a deliberate margin around social media post designs on the grid. Unlike standard full-frame designs that bleed edge-to-edge and blend into the endless feed, framed posts create natural breathing room that instantly commands visual attention, elevates brand premiumness, and effectively stops the scroll.
